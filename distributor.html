Who can enquire about distribution?

Retailers, wholesalers, hospitality businesses and people exploring distribution opportunities are welcome to introduce themselves using the application form.

What information should I provide?

Tell us your business name, contact details, location, business type and the products you are interested in. Include any questions about the area you serve.

What are the prices and minimum order quantities?

Pricing, minimum quantities, product availability and delivery arrangements are confirmed directly by our sales team. Please include your location and products of interest in your enquiry.

Does an application confirm my distributorship?

No. An application starts a conversation. Any appointment and commercial arrangements need to be agreed with our team.
