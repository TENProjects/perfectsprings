When you contact us

Our enquiry forms collect the contact details, product selection and message you provide. Distributor applications also collect your business details and location. Please do not include sensitive personal information in your message.

How your information is used

The information is used to receive, assess and respond to your enquiry. It is sent to the Perfect Springs sales inbox through the configured email service. Enquiry consent does not subscribe you to marketing.

Technical protection

The enquiry service uses a temporary form token, anti-spam checks and rate limiting. Technical identifiers may be used temporarily to prevent abuse. Form contents are not included in website analytics events.

Questions and requests

For questions about how your enquiry is handled, or to request access, correction or deletion, email perfectspringwatersales@gmail.com. Ask our team for details of applicable retention and handling practices.
